# Refund Policy

## Overview
We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are eligible for a refund, this document outlines our refund processing timelines, methods, and criteria.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Refunds are issued for returned items meeting our Return Policy guidelines.
- Cancellations processed prior to order dispatch receive an automatic 100% refund.
- Damaged, defective, or incorrect items qualify for immediate replacement or full refund including original shipping charges.
- Delayed orders where shipment is confirmed lost or transit exceeds 14 business days beyond the projected delivery window qualify for a full refund or free reshipment.

## Refund Processing Timeline
- **Credit Card / Debit Card**: Once approved, refunds appear on your credit or debit statement within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your card issuer.
- **PayPal / Digital Wallets**: Processed within 24 to 48 hours.
- **Store Credit / Gift Cards**: Available immediately upon return inspection.

## Partial Refunds & Restocking Fees
- We do not charge restocking fees on standard returns initiated within the 30-day return window.
- If items are returned missing original packaging, accessories, or manuals, a partial refund deduction (up to 15%) may be applied.
- Original expedited shipping fees (e.g. Next-Day or 2-Day Air) are non-refundable unless the delay or return is due to carrier fault or company error.

## Delayed Orders and Transit Guarantees
- If your tracking status has not updated for more than 5 consecutive business days, our support team can initiate a carrier trace.
- If an order is delayed due to severe weather, customs, or logistical bottlenecks, you may request a refund once the parcel is returned to sender or confirmed lost by the carrier.

## Contacting Billing Support
For refund status inquiries or discrepancies, please have your Order ID (e.g., ORD-1001) ready when contacting our support assistant or emailing billing@example.com.
