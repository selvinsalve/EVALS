# Return Policy

## 30-Day Return Window
Customers may request a return within 30 days of the confirmed delivery date. Returns initiated after 30 days cannot be accepted.

## Condition of Returned Items
To qualify for a full refund:
- Items must be in their original, unused condition with all tags attached.
- All original manufacturer packaging, user manuals, accessories, and documentation must be included.
- Footwear and apparel must be unworn and unwashed.
- Electronics must have personal data cleared and factory settings restored.

## Non-Returnable Items
The following categories are strictly non-returnable for safety, hygiene, and copyright reasons:
- Perishable goods (food, organic beverages, coffee beans).
- Downloadable software products, digital licenses, and activated gift cards.
- Intimate apparel, swimwear, and personal health/grooming items if packaging is opened.
- Custom-engraved or personalized merchandise.

## Return Shipping Procedure
1. Locate your Order ID (e.g., ORD-1002) in the portal or chat with our AI Order Assistant.
2. If the return is due to our error (defective, damaged, or incorrect item), we provide a prepaid prepaid return shipping label.
3. For buyer's remorse or preference changes, the customer is responsible for the return shipping cost ($6.99 flat-rate label deducted from refund).
4. Drop off the packaged parcel at any authorized carrier drop-off location (UPS or FedEx depending on label).

## Return Inspection Timeline
- Once our warehouse receives the returned package, inspection is completed within 2 business days.
- You will receive an automated email notification confirming whether your return has been accepted and refund initiated.
