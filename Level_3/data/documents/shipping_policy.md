# Shipping Policy & Delivery Rates

## Shipping Methods & Delivery Timelines
We offer three reliable delivery tiers across the continental United States:
- **Standard Ground Shipping**: 3 to 5 business days ($4.99 flat-rate; FREE on orders over $50.00).
- **Expedited Express Shipping**: 2 business days ($12.99 flat-rate).
- **Overnight Priority Shipping**: 1 business day ($24.99 flat-rate; order must be placed before 1:00 PM EST).

## Order Processing & Dispatch Timelines
- Orders placed Monday through Friday before 2:00 PM EST are typically processed and dispatched within 24 hours.
- Orders placed on weekends or observed national holidays are processed on the next business day.
- Custom configured orders or freight items may require 1 to 2 additional business days for fulfillment.

## Carriers & Tracking Numbers
- We partner with FedEx, UPS, USPS, and DHL Express to ensure rapid and secure delivery.
- As soon as your order leaves our fulfillment center, a tracking number is generated and assigned.
- Tracking numbers typically become active and start showing scans within 12 to 24 hours of dispatch.

## International Shipping
- Currently, standard web checkout supports shipping to the United States, Canada, and select European destinations.
- International shipments may be subject to import duties, taxes, and customs handling fees imposed by the destination country. These charges are the recipient's responsibility unless duties prepaid (DDP) was selected at checkout.

## Address Corrections & Forwarding
- Delivery addresses can only be modified while the order status is "PLACED" or "CONFIRMED".
- Once the order transitions to "SHIPPED", address changes cannot be guaranteed and may require an intercept fee charged directly by the carrier.
