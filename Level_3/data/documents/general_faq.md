# General Customer Support FAQ

## How do I check the status of my order?
You can inquire directly with our AI Order Assistant by typing "Where is my order?" or providing your Order ID (such as `ORD-1001`). You can also view all historical orders in your account portal.

## Can I change my delivery address?
- If your order is in **PLACED** or **CONFIRMED** status, we can update your delivery address prior to fulfillment.
- If your order has moved to **SHIPPED** or **OUT_FOR_DELIVERY**, address changes cannot be processed directly by our warehouse. In that scenario, you can use the carrier's package management portal (FedEx Delivery Manager or UPS My Choice) to request parcel redirection or hold at a local facility.

## What should I do if my package arrived with missing items?
- Check your packing slip to see if your items were split into multiple shipments. Each parcel receives its own unique tracking number.
- If your order was marked complete with all items in one package, but an item is missing, please contact support within 7 days of delivery. We will promptly ship out the missing item.

## How do I contact a human customer support agent?
- Our live customer support representatives are available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST.
- You can reach human support by phone at 1-800-555-0199 or via email at support@example.com.

## Are gift receipts or gift wrapping options available?
- Yes, gift messaging and packing slips without price information can be selected during checkout.
