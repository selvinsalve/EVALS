# Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Item Policy

## Immediate Guarantee
We take extensive care in packaging your orders. However, if your order arrives damaged during transit, has defective parts, or includes items different from what you ordered, we guarantee a swift resolution at zero additional cost to you.

## Reporting Period
- **Transit Damage / Missing Items**: Please report within 7 days of package delivery.
- **Defective / Non-Working Electronics**: Please report within 14 days of receipt (covered under manufacturer warranty thereafter).

## Steps to Take If Your Order Arrived Damaged
1. **Take Photos**: Capture clear photographs of the damaged shipping box, carrier shipping label, and the damaged product itself.
2. **Contact Support**: Message our AI Order Assistant with your Order ID (e.g. ORD-1001) or email support@example.com with the subject line "Damaged Order - [Your Order ID]".
3. **Keep Original Packaging**: Retain the original shipping box, bubble wrap, and all components until the claim is reviewed. Carriers may inspect damaged packaging.

## Resolution Options
- **Free Replacement**: We will ship a brand-new replacement item via expedited shipping immediately upon claim submission.
- **Full Refund**: If you prefer not to receive a replacement, or if the item is currently out of stock, we will issue a full refund including original shipping charges.
- **No Return Required**: In most minor damage or crushed box cases, you will not be required to ship the broken item back. If return is required, we provide a prepaid prepaid carrier label.
