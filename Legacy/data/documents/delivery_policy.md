# Delivery Policy & Missing Packages

## Delivery Attempts & Signature Requirements
- Most standard parcel deliveries do not require a direct signature and will be deposited in a safe, secure location (front porch, building lobby, or parcel locker).
- High-value orders exceeding $300.00 require a direct adult signature upon delivery to prevent theft.
- If no recipient is available to sign, the carrier (FedEx or UPS) will leave a delivery door tag and make up to three consecutive delivery attempts before holding the package at a nearby pickup center for 5 days.

## Delayed Orders
- When an order encounters transit delays (due to weather phenomena, natural disasters, or carrier sort facility backups), carrier tracking will update with status "DELAYED".
- Estimated delivery dates automatically adjust based on carrier network telemetry.
- If your order has been delayed for more than 4 business days past its original projected delivery date without tracking movement, please alert our support assistant to initiate a priority carrier inquiry.

## What to Do If Tracking Shows "Delivered" But Package Is Missing
If carrier tracking indicates your parcel was delivered but you cannot find it:
1. **Check Surrounding Areas**: Check back doors, garages, porches, bushes, apartment package rooms, and with neighbors.
2. **Review Carrier Delivery Photo**: Check your tracking link for delivery confirmation photos left by the delivery driver.
3. **Wait 24 Hours**: Carriers occasionally scan packages as delivered a few hours prior to actual drop-off.
4. **File a Missing Package Report**: If the package has not arrived within 24 hours of the delivery timestamp, inform our support assistant or support team. We will immediately open a carrier lost-in-transit claim and arrange a replacement shipment or full refund.
