# Payment Methods FAQ

## What payment methods are supported?
We accept a wide range of safe and encrypted payment options:
- **Major Credit and Debit Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, and JCB.
- **Digital Wallets**: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal Express Checkout.
- **Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL)**: Klarna and Afterpay (available for eligible orders between $35.00 and $1,500.00 split across 4 interest-free installments).
- **Store Credit & Gift Cards**: Digital gift cards issued by our store can be redeemed directly at checkout.

## When is my card charged?
- When you place an order, an authorization hold is immediately placed on your card for the total order amount.
- The actual charge is settled when your order is packed and dispatched from our fulfillment center.

## Are sales taxes applied?
- Applicable state and local sales taxes are calculated automatically at checkout based on the destination shipping address.

## Can I change my payment method after placing an order?
- Due to strict PCI-DSS payment compliance standards and data encryption, payment methods cannot be edited after an order is submitted.
- If you need to use an alternate card, please cancel the order (if still in PLACED or PROCESSING status) and place a new order using your desired payment method.

## Payment Security
- All transactions are processed using 256-bit TLS encryption.
- We never store raw credit card numbers or CVV codes on our servers.
